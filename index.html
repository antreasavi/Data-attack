    How to Play

    • Hover the Start Point box to begin.
    • A colored circle drops at a random moment.
    • Soft red or blue: hit that color once.
    • Strong red or blue: hit both boxes of that color.
    • Orange: hit the orange box.
    • Wrong box costs points and resets your streak.
    • Faster reactions score more points.
